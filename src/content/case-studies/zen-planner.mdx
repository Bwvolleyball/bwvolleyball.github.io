From November 2018 to May 2019 I was a Software Engineer at Zen Planner, where I moved the apps from EC2 onto Kubernetes on AWS and tightened the delivery pipeline around them.

## Where it started

Legacy deployment targets and brittle pipelines slowed delivery and observability.

## The move to Kubernetes

I migrated the applications onto AWS-hosted Kubernetes with health probes and monitoring, so observability was in place from the start.

## Faster feedback

I improved CI/CD with custom git hooks and Jenkins pipelines, which hardened delivery and gave engineers faster feedback.

## The people

I mentored teams on Spring Boot and Java patterns.

## The result

Higher deployment reliability, clearer visibility, and faster feedback loops for engineers.
