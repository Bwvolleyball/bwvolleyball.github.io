I joined Promontory MortgagePath as a Software Engineer in May 2019, became a Senior Software Engineer in February 2021, and stayed through December 2021. The headline is the release cadence: from one release every three months to one every week.

## Building the product

As a Software Engineer I built event-sourced Spring Boot services with Axon, in Kotlin and Java. I evolved our REST and GraphQL APIs with backward compatibility, so consumers stayed safe as they changed. I also delivered enhancements to the Angular and TypeScript web app with our UX partners, and mentored teams on Spring, Java, and Kotlin best practices.

## The problem: quarterly releases

Three-month release cycles with brittle environments blocked rapid delivery and feedback.

## What changed

- Rebuilt the pipelines and tightened the promotion quality gates.
- Uplifted the Kubernetes development clusters.
- Standardized Gradle builds to remove drift, including leading migrations from Maven to Gradle with the Kotlin DSL.
- Ran architecture reviews to align services with platform standards and reliability targets.

## The result

Teams moved from quarterly to weekly releases with higher confidence and predictable rollouts:

- Release cadence improved 12× (3 months → 1 week).
- Reduced environment drift and rollout incidents.

That work, along with mentoring, earned me the Lightkeeper Award in Q1 2021. I wrote about the habits behind it in [Ship faster without breaking teams](/writing/delivery-cadence).
